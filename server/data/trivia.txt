# question per line:   difficulty | question | answer | answer | answer | answer
# Difficulty is 1 (easy, worth 1 point) to 5 (hard, worth 5). Mark the correct answer with a
# leading "*". Answer order doesn't matter: answers are shuffled when the question is asked.
# Each category needs at least 5 questions per difficulty (checked by the tests); more means
# more fresh boards in a night. Some Bible, History and Sports questions are adapted from
# OpenTriviaQA (https://github.com/uberspot/OpenTriviaQA, CC BY-SA 4.0).
